# Source records

Replace this scaffold record with a selective, fact-aligned account of one consequential exchange between intent and reality. Preserve:

- the desired effect or concern carried into investigation;
- the checked target facts and supporting evidence;
- the effect those facts have on the intent;
- the resulting decision, changed state, or unresolved branch.

A verified no-change result can be valuable provenance. When it can prevent repeated work or a tempting mistake, record what was checked, why the alternative was rejected, and what new evidence would reopen it. This does not imply a state or realization change.

Keep user authority and Agent reality claims attributable where they matter, but do not structure the record as a chat transcript or two disconnected speaker summaries. Once selected by state and committed or locked, do not rewrite it to make a later interpretation look inevitable; append a correction or superseding decision.
