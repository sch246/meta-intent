## What this decides

1. The map is generated, not primitive. Maintainability is the question of what its generating set is.
2. The generating set is traceability and maintained authority: keeping the "why", and keeping a way to iterate it. It bottoms out in the occurrence of facts and in what was learned from those facts.
3. The complexity of this package comes from one choice: authority is made explicit instead of being left implicit in a conversation. The user names that cost, and names it as the problem of how to create an agent.

## Consequence for the razor

`SRC-2026-09-03-INSTALLATION-MAP-AS-IRREDUCIBLE-CORE` established that a capability the map cannot reach is not delivered, and that material no route has to reach holds no normative authority. This record supplies the route that the authority machinery sits on: the map has to be iterated, iteration has to be traceable to occurrences and authority, so provenance, attribution and derivation-basis requirements are reached by the map's own maintenance route rather than being decoration.

This does not license unlimited machinery. The user identifies explicit authority as the source of the complexity; nothing here fixes how much of it the generator actually requires.

## Scope of this record

Intent clarification. It adds no acceptance criterion, retires no `IP-*`, `META-*`, constraint or open tension, and adopts no protocol successor. It records one new open tension: the minimum explicit-authority machinery under which the map remains regenerable from its generator.
