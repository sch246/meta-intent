## Source statement

> 协议本来就是最中心化的去中心，协议内是完全的中心，但别人也可以看不爽自己创建协议，我自己创协议的话自然要完全的中心

## Authoritative clarification

1. Centrality is relative to one protocol, not to the whole ecosystem. A protocol has one fully centralized canonical meaning; this is a constitutive property of being a protocol rather than a temporary governance compromise.
2. Ecosystem-level decentralization occurs when someone creates or forks a distinct protocol with its own center. Disagreement need not be resolved by distributing authority inside the original protocol.
3. A fork may be a proposal to the original center, but it is also an expected exit path when its author intends to maintain different canonical meaning. That outcome is not inherently a failure of the original protocol.
4. The user created `sch246/meta-intent` and retains complete semantic authority over this protocol lineage. Technical contributors and reviewers do not acquire that authority through access, implementation, or agreement.

The earlier phrase “not a temporary compromise” was an Agent interpretation not established by the quotation then available. The new statement now resolves the underlying point, but on the more precise protocol-versus-ecosystem basis above.

## Stable and variable parts

This definition has no ordinary empirical reopening condition. A future user authority event may revise the definition, but implementation evidence alone cannot do so. What remains variable is who holds or may inherit a protocol's center and how that authority is authenticated, delegated, succeeded, or exercised through any future quorum.
